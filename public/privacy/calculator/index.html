Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Calculator. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Calculator app handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Our Calculator app is designed to work without collecting personally identifiable information.

  • We do not collect your name.
  • We do not collect your email address.
  • We do not collect your phone number.
  • We do not collect your contacts.
  • We do not collect your calculation history for external use.

How the App Works

Calculator performs calculations directly on your device. The calculations you enter are processed locally within the app.

Your calculation data is not transmitted to our servers.

Data Storage

The app does not maintain a personal database containing your information. Any temporary calculation data is handled locally on your device.

Third-Party Services

Calculator does not share your personal information with third-party services.

If future versions of the application use third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Security

Since the app does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user database maintained by us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
